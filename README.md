
# E-Commerce Sales Analysis – Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive E-Commerce Sales Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance, profitability, product performance, regional sales, customer segments, and shipping methods.

The objective of this project is to transform e-commerce sales data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales and profit performance.
- Analyze sales performance across different product categories.
- Identify the top-performing and underperforming products.
- Compare sales performance across different regions and states.
- Analyze sales distribution by customer segment.
- Understand sales contribution by shipping type.
- Track year-to-date performance and year-over-year changes.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis
- Interactive Dashboard & Slicers

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales by Category

Analyzes YTD sales performance across:

- Office Supplies
- Furniture
- Technology

Office Supplies is the highest-performing category with approximately **$6.92M** in YTD sales.

### 2. Top 5 Products by YTD Sales

Identifies the five products generating the highest YTD sales, helping understand which products contribute most to overall revenue.

### 3. Bottom 5 Products by YTD Sales

Highlights the lowest-performing products based on YTD sales and helps identify products that may require further business analysis.

### 4. YTD Sales by Region

Provides a regional breakdown of sales across:

- West
- East
- Central
- South

The **West region contributes the highest share of YTD sales at approximately 32.22%**.

### 5. Sales by State

A geographical visualization is used to analyze sales performance across different U.S. states and regions.

### 6. YTD Sales by Shipping Type

Shows the contribution of different shipping methods:

- Standard Class
- Second Class
- First Class

Standard Class contributes the largest share of sales at approximately **60.51%**.

### 7. Customer Segment Analysis

The dashboard allows sales performance to be analyzed across:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

Interactive segment filters make it easier to compare business performance across customer groups.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- The dashboard generated **$11.53M in YTD sales** and **$1.34M in YTD profit**.
- Overall YTD profit margin stands at **11.58%**.
- **Office Supplies** is the largest sales category with approximately **$6.92M** in YTD sales.
- The **West region** contributes the highest regional sales share at **32.22%**.
- **Standard Class** is the most widely contributing shipping type with **60.51%** of YTD sales.
- The dashboard identifies both top and bottom-performing products, enabling focused product-level analysis.
- Year-over-year indicators help identify areas of growth and decline in sales, quantity, profit, and profit margin.
